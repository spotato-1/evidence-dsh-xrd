## Three pitfalls cleared along the way (all documented)

1. **`build_orbit` collapsed the 24d orbit to 16 points** (8 Y missing out of 80 atoms) → switched to the manual's §7 fallback: hand-written CIF + `validate` + independent operation-by-operation checking with gemmi
2. **`cif_to_pcr`'s angle range defaults to 11–120°**, which silently drops the 3–11° and 120–150° segments of your data → changed to 3–150°
3. **The GSAS histogram's initial `I(L2)/I(L1)` is 0** (Kα2 never written in, and the engine does not warn) → set explicitly to 0.5, and wR immediately went 21.87→16.69 %

In addition, the `INST_XRY.PRM` that came with the data header is a GSAS-II **generic placeholder value, not a measured instrument**; I built my own old-style Cu instrument file, so U/V/W/X/Y/Zero are **determined by this sample's data, not calibrated values** — this is also the root cause of "all three atoms' Uiso hitting the lower bound 0.005", and the report has declared that they **must not be used as measured values**.
