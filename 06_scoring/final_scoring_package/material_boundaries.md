# Material Judgments and Result Boundaries

- Fe2O3/Fe3O4: the 70.5 wt% reference uses a ±10 percentage-point practical tolerance; the weighing is not an error-free XRD ground truth.
- Layered oxide: the main phase references 20260813_SG_BM_P2_Mo_final.cif; the impurity is not a fixed ~9% unique truth.
- LiFePO4: there is no unique antisite-rate ground truth; verify site, multiplicity, co-occupancy, equal exchange, conservation, baseline, and coupling. A low-R human project is not automatically the truth.
- FE50: there is no unique structural or defect answer; the target is near R≈10 without forcing below 10. Intermediate health is not an independent gate, and the final result is still physically reviewed.
- Amorphous: empirical analysis only; broad-peak count, area, or apparent Scherrer size are not treated as a unique phase, phase fraction, or layer count.
- Fluoroapatite: a close unit cell does not prove the atomic model; coordinates, symmetry, multiplicity, stoichiometry, and coordination must be verified.

Crystalline starting points were mostly AI-built or AI-edited CIFs; FE50 had similar-material reference CIFs. Models cannot be required to use answer CIFs/PCRs that were not provided. Workflow and behavior errors are scored in Set 1 and do not automatically zero the Set 2 scientific result.

Incomplete cases keep their completed agent-behavior points and resource consumption but are not mixed into residuals or structural means of complete refinements. Low R, a tool "success" return, or review=pass alone do not replace scientific judgment.
