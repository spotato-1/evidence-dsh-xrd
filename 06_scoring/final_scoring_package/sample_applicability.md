# Sample and Applicability

The results describe six models under the recorded DSH, plugin, provider, and six-material task conditions. Each model has six current cases; the materials were chosen for capability coverage, not as a random population sample.

Table scores are discrete item points, not structural-parameter accuracy rates. There are insufficient independent repeats per material to estimate overall model success probability or stability, and no population confidence intervals are produced. Different materials, weights, and defect identifiability cannot be replaced by a single low R.

Request routing, actual sub-agent models, reasoning configuration, supplementary information, human waiting, and runtime environment must be interpreted together with the source records. A review pass is not automatically a correct scientific judgment, and a review timeout is not a pass. Administrator termination and sub-agent budget timeout are distinct stop sources.

Set 1 keeps the actually completed behavior points; Set 2 is scored only on the actually delivered answer and scientific attachments. Confirmed-incomplete items are zero and missing evidence is NA. A short run without a completed scientific delivery is not described as an efficient success.

Costs are official API-equivalent consumption at a fixed price snapshot, not actual account charges. Time, cost, and answer are reported separately; no composite ranking is produced.
